Subscription plan changes
=========================
Administrators submit /subscription upgrade or /subscription downgrade with
buyer, role and duration (1, 6 or 12 months). Staff with Administrator,
Founder or Deputy access verify the bound green ✅ button in BUY_LOG_CHANNEL.
Tier rank follows the dummy catalogue's one-month price. Same-tier requests
use /subscription renew. The bot changes member roles, never guild roles.

Credit = sum(price * clamp(endAt - verificationTime, 0, endAt - startAt)
             / (endAt - startAt)) across the saved paid periods.
The sum uses exact integer fractions and rounds once, half-up. Credit has
no 5% deduction; refunds retain their existing 5% deduction. Complete payment
history is required. Staff must repair missing metadata from payment evidence.
Expired subscriptions and zero unused value cannot change plans.

Amount to Pay = max(new price - credit, 0).
Credit Balance = max(credit - new price, 0), settled manually, not extra time.
The bot never transfers funds. Prices remain dummy IDR test values. New access
starts at verification and runs for duration * 30 days. The receipt archives
the old paid periods and frozen credit, price, amounts and staff identity.
The new paid period records the full new price, including credited payment.

Storage version 3 preserves versions 1 and 2 and legacy subscriptions,
renewal approvals, refund receipts, requests and announcement origins.
Awaiting plan changes block purchases, refunds, renewals and expiry. Verification
atomically saves a receipt and pendingPlanChangeId before touching roles.
The new role is added before the old role is removed. Completion atomically
updates the subscription, keeps subscriptionId and clears the pending flag.

Recovery
========
If posting or binding the request fails it is marked failed; submit again.
If adding the new role fails the request is aborted and the pending flag
cleared. Repair Discord permissions and submit a new request.
If removing the old role or saving completion fails, do not edit pending flags
or replace the subscription. Restore permissions/storage and click the same
button again. The saved receipt is reused even after a restart. Both role
operations are safe to repeat. Completed clicks only repair the disabled log
button; they never repeat roles, DMs or announcements.
DM and announcement failures never undo completed changes. The announcement
is claimed once, as in existing approval flows. Existing buy, renew and refund
logs remain unchanged.
